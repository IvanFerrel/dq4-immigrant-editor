How to use

  1. Open your save (.sav from DeSmuME/melonDS/RetroArch, or DeSmuME's .dsv).
  2. Pick an immigrant and edit it, or create new ones (max 24, 3 pages of 8).
  3. "Fill up to 24" copies your immigrant into empty slots, each with a different ID.
  4. Click "Save" and replace your save file with the downloaded one (same name).
